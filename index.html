Odia Calendar – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Odia Calendar ("the app") is an Odia calendar showing English and Odia dates, tithi, nakshatra, festivals and Odisha Government holidays. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no sign-in or account, and the app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.

Information stored on your device

The app saves one setting on your phone: your chosen display language (English or Odia), so it is remembered the next time you open the app. This setting never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Internet and permissions

All calendar information (dates, tithi, nakshatra, festivals and holiday lists) is calculated or stored inside the app. The app works offline and does not send any data over the internet. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files or other personal data.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.
